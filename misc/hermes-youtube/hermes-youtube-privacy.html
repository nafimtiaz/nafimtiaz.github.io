Hermes YouTube – Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Hermes YouTube is a personal, self-hosted assistant that helps its owner track and grow their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public: the only person who signs in to it is the owner of the channels it reports on.

Hermes YouTube uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

When you sign in with Google, Hermes YouTube asks for two read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly – your channel's details, videos, playlists, and recent subscribers whose subscriptions are public.
  • yt-analytics.readonly – your channel's YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and subscribers gained or lost.

It cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube, and it has no access to any other Google service such as Gmail, Drive, Photos or Calendar.

Separately from sign-in, it reads publicly available YouTube data, such as trending videos and search results, using an API key.

How the data is used

  • To save daily snapshots of your channel and video statistics so growth can be tracked over time.
  • To send you reports and answer your questions about your channel, for example a daily digest and a weekly strategy note, through a private Telegram bot that only you can message.
  • To suggest video ideas, titles and improvements based on your channel's own performance and on public trends in your niche.

To write those reports, the relevant statistics are sent to an AI language model provider (currently OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to), which processes them to generate the reply. The data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Where the data is stored

  • Statistics are stored in a database on the owner's own computer or home server.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored on that same machine, readable only by the owner's user account. It is never committed to source control or uploaded to cloud storage.
  • Generated reports may be backed up to the owner's own Google Drive. Credentials are excluded from that backup.

Sharing

Data is not shared with anyone other than the AI model provider described above, and is never sold or transferred to third parties for any other purpose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes YouTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke Hermes YouTube's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page (also reachable via Google security settings). The stored token stops working immediately.
  • Stored statistics can be deleted at any time by deleting the database file; data stored on the owner's machine can be removed on request to the contact below.

Changes
